# Palmico Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 17, 2026_

Palmico ("we", "us") is a social prediction game. This policy describes what
we collect, why, and the controls you have. Contact:
**palmicosocial@gmail.com**.

## What we collect

| Data | Purpose | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in identity (Apple or Google) | Account creation and authentication | Yes |
| Username, profile photo | Your identity in the app | Username yes, photo optional |
| Real name | Shown only to your pals, never to anyone else | **Optional** |
| Phone number | Contact matching to find pals | **Optional — never required** |
| Contacts | Matching contacts already on Palmico, only if you grant contacts permission | Optional, fully skippable |
| Predictions, bets, chat messages, badges | Core app functionality | Created by you |
| Coin ledger (virtual currency history) | Balance integrity and transaction history | Generated by use |
| Device push token | Notifications you've enabled | Optional |
| Purchase records | Crediting micoin purchases made through Apple | Only if you buy |

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
- Micoins have no cash value; we collect no financial account information —
  purchases are processed entirely by Apple.

## Who we share with

Palmico runs on infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf:
**Supabase** (database and authentication) and **Apple** (sign-in, purchases,
push notifications). We may disclose information if required by law.

## Your visibility controls

Profile visibility (public or pals-only), per-category visibility
(forward-only — making a category public never exposes past predictions),
Crew visibility on your profile, and per-badge visibility are all controlled
by you in **Settings → Privacy & Visibility**.

## Retention & deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any
time in **Settings → Account → Delete account**. Deletion removes your
profile, predictions, bets, coin history, badges, messages, and device
tokens, and revokes your Sign in with Apple token. Residual copies in
encrypted backups are purged on the provider's standard backup cycle (up to
30 days).

## Your rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or
erase your personal data. The in-app deletion flow covers erasure; for
anything else, email **palmicosocial@gmail.com** and we'll respond within 30 days.

## Age

Palmico is rated 18+. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly
collect data from anyone under 18.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the app before they take
effect.
