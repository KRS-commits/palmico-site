# Palmico.

**Call it on your friends.**

Palmico is a free social prediction game where pals make lighthearted
predictions about each other — *shows up late*, *cancels plans last minute*,
*cries during the vows* — make their picks, and earn points for the calls
they get right. Points are just a score for your Crew's leaderboard.
