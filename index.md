# Palmico.

**Bet on your friends. Literally.**

Palmico is a social prediction game where pals make lighthearted predictions
about each other — *shows up late*, *cancels plans last minute*, *cries during
the vows* — and play with **micoins**, a just-for-fun virtual currency.
Micoins have no cash value and cannot be redeemed, transferred for value, or
withdrawn.
